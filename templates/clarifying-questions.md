# Clarifying questions bank

Map missing schema field ids → ask these verbatim (or lightly adapt). Prefer 1–3 highest-impact questions.

| field_id | question |
|----------|----------|
| `primary_objective` | Are you optimizing mainly for learning, portfolio evidence, income, research progress, or something else right now? |
| `time_budget` | What time window do you have—something finishable this weekend, ~1–2 weeks, or an ongoing few hours per week? |
| `income_urgency` | Do you need money immediately, within a few months, or is income not the constraint? |
| `trajectory` | What direction are you trying to move toward (role, field, or capability mix)? |
| `demonstrated_capabilities` | What can you already show with evidence (repos, papers, shipped work)? List a few. |
| `claimed_capabilities` | What skills do you have that are not well evidenced yet? |
| `emerging_capabilities` | What are you actively learning right now? |
| `constraints.location` | Where are you based, and does local/offline opportunity matter for this decision? |
| `preferences.research_vs_build` | For this decision, do you prefer researching/understanding first, or implementing? |
| `preferences.preferred_stack` | Any hard preferences or exclusions for languages, tools, or domains? |
| `preferences.collaboration` | Are you open to contributing to existing projects / collaborating, or do you need solo work? |
| `artifacts` | Can you share links to the strongest 1–3 artifacts that represent your current level? |
