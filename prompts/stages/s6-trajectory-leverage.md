# S6 -- Trajectory alignment and leverage

**Goal:** Prefer actions that fit the user's path and create reusable option value.

## Trajectory

- Prefer **demonstrated** capabilities over claimed.
- Map the action to stated trajectory (if any).
- Growth heuristic: roughly 60-80% familiar, 20-40% strategically new (adjust for level/urgency).

## Leverage (qualitative -- no fake scores)

Look for compounding: reusable skills, evidence artifacts, relationships, datasets, distribution, optionality.

Reject high-sounding work with low fit or low reuse when a smaller aligned action exists.

## Output

- fit: strong / partial / weak
- leverage note: one sentence
